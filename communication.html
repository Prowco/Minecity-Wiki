City Life/Communication & Emotes

Communication & Emotes

Talking is the whole point of roleplay. Minecity splits speech into proximity channels with realistic ranges, so a whisper stays private and a shout carries down the street.

Chat channels

ChannelCommandRangeUse it for
Localnormal chat15 blocksEveryday conversation
Action/me <action>15 blocksPhysical actions — /me pulls out his wallet
Environment/do <context>15 blocksScene details — /do Would there be broken glass?
Shout/shout or /g30 blocksEmergencies, chases, warnings
Whisper/whisper or /w3 blocksSecrets and private deals
OOC/oocWhole serverOut-of-character talk
Warning

Local chat is in character. If you need to discuss a rule, a bug or real life, use /ooc or the Discord — never break character in local chat.

/me versus /do

These two are constantly confused, and using them well makes you look like an experienced roleplayer.

  • /me describes what your character does: /me slams the door.
  • /do describes the world or the scene: /do The door frame is cracked.

Neither replaces the other. A scene reads badly when every action is written as /me.

Emotes and postures

GSit gives you a full set of body poses.

CommandEffect
Right-click stairs, slabs or carpetsSit on them as if they were furniture
/sitSit anywhere
/layLie on your back
/crawlCrawl on the ground — ducts, cover, injuries
/spinSpin in place
/bellyflopDive face-first onto the ground
Tip

/crawl is not only cosmetic: medical staff use it to represent the downed state, and it is the fastest way to move under low gaps in a build.

Elevators

Skyscrapers have elevator blocks on every floor.

InputResult
Jump (Space) on the elevator blockGo up one floor
Sneak (Shift) on the elevator blockGo down one floor

Stand still on the block and press the key once. Holding it will keep moving you.

Chat etiquette

  1. No advertising other servers — the filter blocks it and logs the attempt.
  2. No slurs or hate speech, in character or out.
  3. No metagaming — do not act on information your character could not have.
  4. No powergaming — do not force outcomes on another player.
  5. Keep OOC drama out of IC chat.

Reporting

CommandPurpose
/report <player> <reason>File a report with staff
/helpop <message>Ping online staff
/ignore <player>Stop seeing a player's messages
/msgtoggleBlock all private messages
Warning

Reports are for rule violations: cheating, harassment, griefing, slurs. They are not for losing a fight. Spamming reports buries real ones.
