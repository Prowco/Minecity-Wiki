Crime, Drugs & Gangs

Crime is a career path, not a griefing licence. The server rewards planning — crews, drills, getaway routes — and punishes improvisation with stars, prison and lost equipment.

Bank robberies

KoyaRobbery drives the heists.

RequirementDetail
ToolVault Drill (special item)
Drilling time50 seconds
Alarmcustom.robbery siren
ConsequenceAssailants receive 3 wanted stars automatically
Police responseLive coordinates broadcast to every officer

The heist loop

  1. Assemble a crew. One person drilling, at least one watching the door.
  2. Bring the drill. The item is consumed or required by the vault door.
  3. Start drilling. The alarm fires and the police are told where you are.
  4. Hold the position for the full duration.
  5. Leave before the perimeter closes.
Important

Fifty seconds of standing still while the entire police force knows your coordinates. Bring more people than you think you need.

Drugs

The narcotics economy has three branches.

Cannabis

  • Grow from seeds.
  • Harvest and dry the buds.
  • Roll joints using paper and a lighter.
  • Sold to buyers or other players.

Tobacco

  • Process leaves.
  • Produce cigarette packs.
  • Lower value, lower risk, always in demand.

Chemical substances

ProductEffectDownside
CocaineSpeed burstNausea, addiction
MushroomsHallucinationsUnpredictable
LSDVisual distortionLong recovery
MethamphetamineStrength and speedSevere crash, addiction
Warning

All substances cause side effects and can create dependency. In roleplay that is the point. Do not treat them as free buffs.

Staying hidden

  • Never produce in your own apartment. Regions are logged and searchable.
  • Rotate locations.
  • Do not carry product in public — a police search finds it.
  • Selling to an undercover officer ends a criminal career fast.

Gangs

Gangs use ClansPlus, with gang configuration also present for ExcellentGangs.

CommandEffect
/clan create <name>Found a criminal organisation
/clan invite <player>Recruit a member
/clan bankShared vault for supplies and weapons
/clan warDeclare a territorial dispute
/clan leaveWalk away
/clan disbandDestroy the gang (leader only)

The gang bank

A shared wallet with no chargebacks. Deposit loot, fund weapons, pay bail.

Tip

Give withdrawal rights sparingly. One greedy member empties a treasury faster than any rival gang.

Territory and war

  • Wars are declared with /clan war.
  • Winning generates rewards for the whole clan.
  • Alliances can be formed and broken — betrayals are excellent roleplay, provided they stay in character.

See Police Department for the full system.

Prison

If arrested:

  • Inventory confiscated
  • Weapons blacklisted inside
  • Teleport commands disabled
  • Sentence served in real minutes

Early release is at staff discretion. Do not spam /helpop.

Rules for criminals

  1. No random crime. Robbing the same player repeatedly is harassment with extra steps.
  2. Value your life. Five officers with rifles is not the moment to draw.
  3. No safe-zone crime. Spawn protection and hospitals are off limits.
  4. No combat logging.
  5. Losing is part of it. Getting caught is a story beat.
Note

Everything here is in-character. Real-world law does not apply in the city, but server rules absolutely do.
