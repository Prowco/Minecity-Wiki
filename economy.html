City Life/Economy & Banking

Economy & Banking

Every system in the city feeds the same currency, managed through Vault. Understanding where money is safe — and where it is not — is the difference between a comfortable character and a broke one.

Cash versus bank

FormWhere it livesSafe?
CashItems in your inventoryNo — lost when robbed or killed
Bank balanceYour accountYes
Gang bankShared clan treasuryYes, but shared

Cash is physical. It occupies inventory slots and it drops with your body.

Warning

If you are carrying more than you would be happy to lose, you are carrying too much. Deposit before every risky trip.

The bank

Branches of the Central Bank are in the city centre, and ATMs are scattered around every district.

/bank
CommandWhat it does
/bankOpens your account panel
/bank deposit <amount>Puts cash into your account
/bank withdraw <amount>Takes cash out
/bank pay <player> <amount>Transfers money to another citizen
/bank loanOpens the loan system
Tip

deposit all and withdraw all are accepted shortcuts in the GUI. Use them after every payday.

Transfers and payments

  • /bank pay <player> <amount> moves money directly between accounts.
  • /pay <player> <amount> is the Essentials equivalent and works the same way.
  • /paytoggle blocks all incoming payments if you are being spammed.

For face-to-face roleplay payments, the friend inventory lets you hand over cash items directly.

Salaries

Salary is paid automatically every 60 minutes of active play. AFK time does not count.

RoleWage
CivilianBasic civic allowance
Paramedic on duty$500 per hour
Police officerState wage, scaled by rank
Citizen level 1+Higher brackets as your level rises

See Citizen Levels for the full salary ladder.

/payday shows when your next payment is due.

Loans

/bank loan opens the credit system.

  • Loans are for citizens with a stable income and a clean record.
  • Interest is charged over the repayment period.
  • Defaulting blocks future credit and can freeze your account.
Note

A loan is a roleplay commitment, not free money. Do not borrow to gamble — that story ends with a repo man, not a jackpot.

The auction house

/ah opens the player marketplace.

CommandEffect
/ahBrowse listings
/ah sell <price>List the item in your hand
/ah expiredRecover expired listings
/ah collectClaim money from sales

Listings run for seven days. Damaged items cannot be listed.

Practical rules

  1. Bank after every payday. No exceptions.
  2. Keep a rent buffer of at least one cycle.
  3. Never carry a full balance into a robbery, a chase or a gunfight.
  4. Do not trust "duplication" offers. They are exploits, rolled back with a ban.
  5. Check /ah prices before selling to a vendor — players often pay more.
