Leisure & Casino

Not everything in the city is work or crime. There is a stadium, a casino and a lottery, and all three are fully functional.

Football — BlockBall

The municipal stadium runs real matches with BlockBall.

FeatureDetail
PhysicsReal ball physics, passing and shooting
GoalsWorking goals with score detection
ScoreboardLive interactive scoreboard
TeamsRed and blue

Join a match:

/blockball join
Tip

Matches are organised by players or staff. If the stadium is empty, start one and announce it in chat — a five-a-side game fills the stands faster than any event poster.

Playing well

  • Pass early; the ball physics punish solo runs.
  • Defend as a team, not as five individual strikers.
  • The goalkeeper is a real position. Someone has to take it.
  • Do not use weapons or vehicles on the pitch.

The casino

/pg

The casino hosts several games in a GUI:

GameFormat
RouletteEuropean wheel, configurable chips
Video pokerDraw poker against the machine
Pocket gamesA rotating set of quick-bet games

Individual game menus are also available:

/roulette

The honest maths

GameHouse edgeRealistic outcome
Roulette~2.7% – 5.3%Slow bleed
Video poker1% – 5%Break-even at best
Quick-bet gamesVariesDesigned to be fun, not profitable

Over enough hands, every game takes money from you. That is what a casino is.

The lottery

/lottery
SettingDetail
Ticket priceFixed per ticket
Tickets per playerLimited
DrawOn a fixed schedule
PotFunded by ticket sales
TaxA percentage of winnings

The lottery is player-funded: more players means a bigger prize, not better odds.

Spending responsibly

  • Budget first. Decide what you can lose, then walk in with exactly that.
  • Never gamble rent money. Great story, terrible week.
  • Bank your winnings immediately — cash on the street is cash for a mugger.
  • Do not borrow in character to gamble. Debt roleplay escalates fast.
Important

Gambling addiction is real outside the game too. If chasing losses in-character is making you miserable out-of-character, stop and take a break. The city will still be there.

Hosting your own event

The best entertainment on a roleplay server is player-run.

  1. Pick something with a clear start time.
  2. Announce it in /ooc and on Discord.
  3. Put up a prize from your own pocket — even a small one draws a crowd.
  4. Ask staff to broadcast it if it is big enough.
Note

Staff-run events usually come with unique rewards. Watch chat for announcements.
