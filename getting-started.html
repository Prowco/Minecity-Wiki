Getting Started

Every player starts as a civilian arriving at the central terminal of the metropolis. No job, no car, no reputation — just a phone, an ID and whatever you can earn in your first hours.

The guided tour

On your first join, a third-person guided tour walks you through the key districts:

  • Police Station — arrests, reports and jail time
  • General Hospital — the EMS corps
  • Central Bank — deposits, withdrawals, loans
  • Dealership — where vehicles are sold
  • Residential Zone — apartments and houses

You can replay it whenever you like:

/tutorial
Tip

Do the tour even if you already know Minecraft. It shows the exact buildings you will need on day one, and the route between them is the fastest way to learn the map.

Your smartphone and SIM

You receive a smartphone in your inventory. It is required for calls, texts, the 911 line and money transfers.

/phone

Your number is 5 digits, generated when your SIM is registered. To share it, hand over the SIM Number item or say it in roleplay.

Note

Only 5-digit numbers exist. A longer number is either bad roleplay or a scam.

Full details on the Phone & GPS page.

Police will ask for /card show during a stop. Refusing is a roleplay decision with consequences.

Money on day one

FormWhere it livesRisk
CashItems in your inventoryLost if robbed or killed
Bank balanceYour account at the Central BankSafe

Salaries are paid every 60 minutes of active play. Civilians get the basic civic allowance; police and paramedics earn their own wages on top.

Warning

Carrying your whole savings in cash is the most common way new players lose everything. Deposit it at an ATM or with /bank before you go anywhere dangerous.

The daily loop

  1. Work — take a job quest, mine, fish or drive a route.
  2. Earn — quest rewards plus your hourly salary.
  3. Bank it — deposit before you travel.
  4. Spend — rent, vehicle, fuel, food, medical bills.
  5. Progress — hours played and capital raise your citizen level.

Staying alive

Two systems kill faster than other players:

  • Hydration and hunger drain constantly, and dehydration damage bypasses normal regeneration. See Hydration & Hunger.
  • Bleeding from gunshots becomes sepsis after three minutes. See EMS & Hospital.

Carry water and a trauma kit. Both are cheap compared to dying.

Roleplay ground rules

  • Stay in character. Local chat is in-character; use /ooc for everything else.
  • No metagaming. If your character did not see or hear it, they do not know it.
  • No random violence. You need an in-character reason to attack anyone.
  • Value your life. Complying at gunpoint is the correct response.
  • Losing is content. Getting arrested, robbed or killed is a story beat.
