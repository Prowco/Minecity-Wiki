Jobs, Mines & Fishing

Honest work pays in Minecity, and it pays consistently. Three systems cover it: mines that respawn, fishing with competitions, and quests handed out by city NPCs.

Mining

Quarries and municipal mines are managed by RealMines and MineResetLite.

FeatureDetail
RegenerationMines reset on a timer, automatically
ResourcesOres and stone, varying by mine
AccessPublic quarries
RiskLow — unless you mine in a disputed area

How a mining run works

  1. Get a pickaxe and a vehicle with cargo space if you have one.
  2. Go to an active quarry.
  3. Mine until your inventory is full.
  4. Sell the ore, or deliver it for a quest.
  5. Repeat — the mine regenerates on its own.
Tip

Bring water. Mining is the longest uninterrupted activity in the city, and dehydration will kill you before a cave does.

Fishing

Fishing is run by EvenMoreFish and is far more than a novelty.

Fishing shop

/emf shop

Sells bait, tackle and buys your catch at vendor prices.

Tournaments

Competitions run on a schedule and pay real money.

  1. A tournament starts and the server is notified.
  2. You have a fixed window to catch as much as possible.
  3. The leaderboard updates live.
  4. The top anglers are paid when it ends.
Note

Only fish caught during the window count. Stockpiling does not work.

Making fishing pay

  • Fish during tournaments, not between them — the prize pool dwarfs vendor prices.
  • Keep rare fish in storage so a death does not wipe a week of work.
  • Fish near home. Travel time is unpaid.
  • Bring water: sitting on a dock for an hour drains hydration fast.

City quests

Quests are handled by QuestsMinecity.

TypeExample
CourierDeliver a package across the city
TransportMove goods without damaging them
ErrandsOdd jobs from NPCs
Discreet contractsWork that does not appear on any ledger

Using the quest board

/quests
  1. Read the objective before accepting.
  2. Accept it — it becomes active.
  3. Complete it in the world; progress tracks automatically.
  4. Claim the reward.
  5. The next tier appears.
Warning

Only a limited number of quests can be active at once. Finish or abandon one before taking another, and note that abandoning usually clears progress.
