EMS & Hospital

Medical care is a real loop in Minecity: you get hurt, you bleed, you go down, and someone has to reach you with the right item before the timer expires.

The downed state

Lethal damage does not kill you instantly.

ConditionBehaviour
Timer60 seconds
PostureLying on the ground, crawl pose, cannot stand
AudioAccelerated heartbeat
VisualsBlindness effects, countdown on screen
On timeoutPermanent death

While downed you can still talk. Tell people where you are.

Important

The 60 seconds are real. If no paramedic reaches you, you die, drop your inventory and respawn. Your wanted stars stay with you.

The revive protocol

  1. A paramedic on duty reaches you.
  2. They must hold a Restoration Vaccine — no vaccine, no revive.
  3. They shift + right-click you.
  4. A GUI appears giving you 30 seconds to authorise a $250 fee.
  5. On acceptance: $250 moves to the paramedic, the vaccine is consumed, and you stand up restored.
Tip

If you cannot pay, say so before accepting. Some medics will treat you anyway, but the transfer needs the money to exist.

Administrative revive

/paramedic revive <player>

Instant, free and requires no vaccine. Requires paramedic.admin.

The emergency radar

/paramedic menu

The central EMS interface shows:

FeatureDetail
Emergency radarEvery downed citizen with exact X, Y, Z
Bleedout timerSeconds remaining for each victim
Compass trackingPoints your compass at the nearest patient
Rapid approachTeleports you roughly 100 blocks away — never on top of them
Medical shopVaccines and supplies at institutional prices

The random offset is deliberate: you still have to run the last stretch.

Tip

Teleporting 100 blocks away and then complaining you cannot find the body is the classic EMS mistake. Use the compass.

Duty kit

/paramedic kit

Claims your on-duty medical kit. /paramedic editkit saves your current inventory as the new default kit.

Wage

Paramedics on duty earn $500 per hour, paid automatically.

Warning

Sepsis is the trap. A gunshot wound is cheap in the first minute and dangerous by the fourth. Treat bleeding immediately.

The pharmacy

/pharmacy

Open to every citizen. Antacids, cough syrup, splints, painkillers, trauma kits and antibiotics are all on the public shelf.

Warning

The Restoration Vaccine is not sold publicly. It is exclusive to authorised paramedics through /paramedic menu.

Conduct expectations

  • You are not a combat medic. Do not run into an active shootout.
  • Triage. If three people are down, the one with seconds left goes first.
  • Do not loot patients. Taking items from someone you revived is theft.
  • Charge fairly. Refusing to treat a dying player over money is a choice with consequences.

Staff notes

Configuration lives in plugins/MinecitySuite/paramedic-config.yml: bleedout time, revive cost and timeout, restore values, cure fee, teleport distance, pharmacy prices and each disease's trigger thresholds.
