City Life/Pets

Pets

Pets are a full virtual-companion system, not vanilla wolves that follow you around. Every pet has hunger, energy, happiness and health, and all of them decay in real time.

Getting a pet

/pet shop

Pets start as babies and grow into adults over several real days. A baby is cheaper to care for but weaker.

The four needs

NeedDecays over timeAt zero
HungerYesStarts losing health
EnergyYes, faster while awakeStarts losing health
HappinessYes, unless played withStarts losing health
HealthOnly from neglectThe pet dies permanently
Warning

Pets die for good. There is no respawn, no insurance and no refund. If you cannot log in regularly, do not buy an expensive one.

Commands

CommandEffect
/petOpens the pet menu — feed, play, sleep, walk
/pet shopBuy a pet
/pet callSummons your active pet
/pet hideSends your pet home, hidden and safe
/pet name <name>Renames your pet
/pet sell <player> <price>Offers the pet to another player
/pet acceptAccepts an incoming sale
/pet cancelCancels a pending sale
/pet releaseFrees the pet permanently

Aliases: /pets, /mascota, /mascotas.

Caring for it

ActionEffect
FeedRestores hunger; costs money
PlayRaises happiness, but you are frozen for a few seconds
WalkRaises happiness for free
SleepStops happiness drain and regenerates energy
Tip

Walking is free happiness. Play is faster but roots you in place — never play with your pet in the middle of a gunfight.

Automatic behaviour

  • After a period of inactivity your pet sits down on its own.
  • Owner damage can be toggled: your own hits either hurt your pet or do nothing.
  • Other players cannot kill your pet by default. If someone does, report it — that is griefing.

Selling a pet

/pet sell Steve 2000

The buyer runs /pet accept and the money moves. This is the only way to recover part of your investment, and it is a legitimate player-to-player market.

Staff notes

  • Configuration: plugins/MinecitySuite/pets-config.yml
  • Feed price, happiness gains, decay rates, AFK timing and the shop catalogue are all editable there.
  • Admin: /pet reload, /pet admin <player> with minesuite.admin.

Should you buy one?

If you…Then
Log in most daysA pet is cheap company and good roleplay

Want a status symbolBuy an expensive pet and show it off
