City Life/Phone & GPS

Phone & GPS

The smartphone is the most important item a citizen owns. It handles calls, texts, contacts, emergency dispatch and money transfers, and it pairs with a GPS overlay that draws a route on the asphalt.

Opening the phone

/phone

You hold a phone item from the moment you spawn. If you lose it, ask staff — it is not craftable.

FeatureWhat it does
ContactsSave names and 5-digit numbers
SMSPrivate text messages to any registered number
911Emergency call with live coordinates
CallsVoice handled through chat
TransfersSend money to a contact

SIM cards and numbers

A phone is useless without a SIM.

CommandWhoWhat it does
/givesimStaffIssues a SIM card
/customsim <number>StaffAssigns a specific 5-digit number
Note

Numbers are exactly 5 digits. There is no area code and no country code — this is a city network, not a real one.

Contacts

  • Add a contact by name and number from the phone menu.
  • Duplicate names or numbers are rejected by the system.
  • You can store both the civic name and the name the person gave you — useful when someone lies about who they are.

The 911 line

The emergency button is the most underused feature in the city.

  1. Open /phone and press 911.
  2. Your live coordinates are broadcast to on-duty police and paramedics.
  3. A call appears in the EMS radar and the police alert feed.
Tip

Calling 911 with your location is faster and more reliable than shouting in chat. It also creates a record, which matters if the scene ends up in a report.

GPS navigation

The GPS draws a trail of glowing particles along the ground toward your destination.

CommandEffect
/gpsOpens the destination menu
/gps start <destination>Starts a route
/gps stopCancels the route

Preconfigured destinations include:

  • Hospital
  • Central Bank
  • Police Station
  • Pharmacy
  • Workshop / garage
  • Dealership
Warning

GPS draws a straight-ish path, not a legal one. It will happily route you through a wall, off a pier or across a runway. Use your eyes, not just the particles.

Combining phone and GPS

The intended loop for a new citizen:

  1. Call a friend or employer with /phone.
  2. Get the address.
  3. Start /gps toward the nearest landmark.
  4. Follow the particles, then use /card show when you arrive.

Privacy

  • Your number is only visible to people you give it to.
  • Staff can read message logs during an investigation — do not use SMS for anything you would not want reviewed.
  • Blocking works: /msgtoggle stops all incoming private messages.
