Public Services/Police Department

Police Department

The police system is the most mechanically detailed part of the city. Stars follow you across deaths, the baton requires real hits, handcuffs physically freeze a suspect, and jail strips your inventory.

Wanted stars

Wanted level runs from 0 to 5 stars. It is displayed in three places at once:

LocationAppearance
NameTagStar icons above the character's head
ChatA prefix such as [★★★☆☆] before the name
TAB listA wanted indicator in the player list
Warning

Stars persist through death. If a suspect dies during an operation, they keep their stars when they respawn or are revived, so they still have to answer for the crime.

How heat is gained

ActionEffect
Committing a violent crimeStars added
Killing a playerSignificant jump
Bank robberyImmediate multi-star alert
Attacking an officerEscalation

How heat is lost

  • Time — stars decay while you keep a low profile.
  • Arrest — serving a sentence clears or reduces the record.
  • Officer discretion — /police removestars for corrections and plea deals.

Equipment

The jail stick

/police jailstick

The baton is not a right-click arrest. To take someone in:

  1. The suspect must be within melee range.
  2. Left-click them.
  3. Land the required number of hits — 2 by default, configurable in police-config.yml.

This exists on purpose: it stops accidental arrests and makes a takedown feel like a fight.

Handcuffs

/police handcuffs
ActionResult
Right-click a playerCuffs them — they cannot move
Right-click a cuffed player againOpens the carry / uncuff menu
Shift + left-click while carryingDrops the carried player

A cuffed suspect can be searched, carried and transported. They stay cuffed until an officer removes them.

Searching

While holding the baton, sneak + right-click a player to open their inventory. They are frozen while you look. This is the legitimate way to find contraband.

Prison

/police jail <player> <minutes>

On entry:

  • Inventory is confiscated and stored
  • Teleport commands are blocked
  • Weapons are blacklisted inside
  • A countdown shows the remaining sentence

Release happens at the configured exit point:

/police setexit

Sets the release location to where the officer is standing.

Note

If a restart drops the release task and a prisoner is stuck, staff can release them manually with /police unjail.

Training dummies

/ms adddummy <name>

Spawns a training NPC with real health. Dummies:

  • Take ballistic and melee damage
  • Bleed
  • Die normally
  • Respawn at their designated spawn point

They are the correct way to train recruits without involving civilians.

Conduct expectations

  1. Announce yourself. "Police! Stop!" before the baton is the difference between an arrest and a random kill.
  2. Escalate, do not execute. A fleeing suspect is not a licence to shoot.
  3. Match force to threat. One star is a fine; five stars is a manhunt.
  4. No OOC arrests. Metagaming an arrest is a staff matter.
  5. Search with cause. Random inventory searches destroy trust in the department.

Staff notes

  • Configuration: plugins/MinecitySuite/police-config.yml
  • Jails are defined under jails: and can be added in-game.
  • stars.max-stars sets the ceiling; police.stick-required-hits sets the arrest combo.
  • Keep keep-wanted-stars-on-death: true in paramedic-config.yml if you want stars to survive death.
  • /police admin exposes the create-kit and maintenance subcommands.
