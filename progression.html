Citizen Levels

Progression in Minecity is measured by Autorank: playtime plus capital in your account. You do not apply for a level — you reach it, and the server promotes you.

Checking your progress

/ar check

Shows your current level and exactly what you still need for the next one.

/ar leaderboard

Shows the most active citizens on the server.

The ladder

LevelRequirementWhat it means
Level 1 — Novato3 hours played + $98 in the bankYou are no longer brand new
Level 2 — Residente12 hours played + $320 in the bankEstablished citizen
Level 3 — Ciudadano IlustreFurther playtime and capitalAdvanced licences and better salaries
Note

Requirements are cumulative and checked automatically. Money in your inventory does not count — only your bank balance does. Deposit before you check.

What levels unlock

UnlockEffect
Salary tiersHigher hourly wage at every level
Advanced licencesAccess to restricted jobs and equipment
Property limitsLarger or additional regions
TrustBetter loan terms, faster staff responses

Salary scaling

Your hourly salary rises with your citizen level. The ladder runs from a basic civic allowance up through progressively higher tiers.

Level bracketSalary trend
Default / Level 1Basic allowance
Levels 2–5Noticeable increase
Levels 6–10Comfortable middle class
Levels 11–15Upper bracket

/payday shows the next payment. AFK time does not count toward playtime or salary.

How to level efficiently

  1. Stay logged in and active. Playtime is the primary gate, and AFK does not count.
  2. Bank your money. Only the bank balance counts toward the capital requirement.
  3. Do job quests. They pay and they keep you active.
  4. Do not die with cash. Every death resets progress toward the capital gate.
  5. Check /ar check weekly so you know what is missing.
Warning

Chasing the capital requirement by carrying cash is counterproductive: one death and you are back to zero. Bank it.

Levels versus roleplay rank

SystemWhat it measures
Autorank levelPlaytime and money — automatic
Job rank (police, EMS)Appointed by staff or a chief
Clan rankSet by your clan leader

They are independent. A level 15 citizen can still be a rookie officer, and a clan leader can be level 3.

Staff notes

  • Autorank data lives in plugins/Autorank/.
  • Requirement paths are defined in Paths.yml, behaviour in Settings.yml.
  • /ar set <player> <level> forces a level — use it only for corrections, and log it.
