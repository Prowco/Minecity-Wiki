City Life/Houses & Apartments

Houses & Apartments

Real estate runs on AdvancedRegionMarket (ARM), wrapped in a custom confirmation menu by MinecitySuite. Every apartment, house and commercial lot is a protected WorldGuard region with a price, an owner and a contract.

The three entry points

CommandOpens
/apartmentsFull property browser — everything available and everything you own
/rentOnly properties for rent
/buyOnly permanent purchases

Rent or buy

RentBuy
Upfront costLowerHigher

Loses the property if you stop payingYesNo

Note

Rent contracts are typically 7 or 30 days. If a contract is not renewed, it expires automatically — the region is reset and resold.

Property signs — the safe way

Every property has a sign in the world. Clicking it never charges you instantly.

Warning

On Minecity, clicking a property sign always opens a confirmation GUI. If money leaves your account the moment you click, that is a bug — report it.

Inside the GUI you can:

  • Check the price, size and time remaining
  • Confirm a rental or a purchase
  • Extend an existing contract by a set number of days
  • See your current contract before committing

Managing a property

From /apartments → Manage:

ActionEffect
ExtendAdds another rental period
InfoRegion bounds, owner, members, time left
MembersLets another player build and access
Sell backReturns the region to the market, if allowed

Adding a tenant

/arm addmember <region> <player>

The region owner can add friends, roommates or employees. They get build and access rights inside that region only.

Tip

Add a roommate only after they have paid. There is no "undo rent" command, and removing someone does not recover items they already took.

Rules that get people evicted

  1. Do not build outside your region. WorldGuard blocks it, and repeated attempts look like griefing.
  2. Do not block roads or spawn exits.
  3. Do not rent a property to store contraband. Regions are logged and can be searched.
  4. Pay your rent. There is no grace period worth relying on.
  5. Do not sublet without the owner's agreement — it is the fastest way to lose a lease.

Commercial and hotel properties

Some regions are flagged as hotels (rented per stay, reset automatically) or commercial (shops and businesses). Commercial lots expect you to keep a vendor or a stocked chest shop; empty storefronts can be reclaimed.

Property as an investment

  • Buy in the city centre: expensive, holds value, close to everything.
  • Rent on the outskirts: cheap, longer commute, more space.
  • Commercial lots pay for themselves only if you actually stock them.
  • A garage matters more than a garden once you own a vehicle.
Warning

Never sink your entire savings into a purchase. Vehicles break, medical bills arrive, and a property you cannot afford to keep is worse than an apartment you can.
