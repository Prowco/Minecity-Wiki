City Life/Hydration & Hunger

Hydration & Hunger

Minecraft's hunger bar is not enough for a roleplay city, so MinecitySuite adds a hydration stat on top. Both bars drain over time, both are restored by consumables, and both reaching zero is lethal.

Checking your stats

/survival status

Shows your exact hydration and hunger percentages in real time.

A HUD element also tracks them while you play, and turns red when either value falls into the critical range.

How fast it drains

Both values fall continuously, faster while sprinting.

StatDrainEmpty from full
HydrationGradual, per minuteRoughly under an hour
HungerSlower than hydrationAround an hour

Sprinting multiplies both drain rates. Walking across the city instead of running cuts your water consumption significantly.

Tip

If you are broke, walk. Water is cheaper than a hospital bill, but walking is free.

What restores hydration

Every drink item is consumed physically from your inventory.

DrinkRestores
Water bottle / bucketMost
MilkHigh
Juice / smoothieHigh
SodaMedium
Coffee, teaMedium
Beer, wine, spiritsLeast
PotionHigh
Warning

Alcohol restores the least hydration of any drink. A beer is a roleplay prop, not a water substitute.

What restores hunger

All food items work, and custom UltimateFood items are supported. Heavier meals restore more than snacks.

Food also synchronises with the vanilla hunger bar, so the shanks above your hotbar stay meaningful.

Physiological collapse

When hydration and hunger both reach 0%:

  1. Your body enters collapse.
  2. You lose half a heart every 10 seconds.
  3. The damage continues until you drink and eat, or until you die.
Important

This damage bypasses normal regeneration. Eating a steak does not save you if your water is at zero — drink first.

Practical survival

  • Carry two water bottles at all times.
  • Refill at any water source: free water is the best deal in the city.
  • Eat before you are hungry, not after.
  • Do not start a long drive, a robbery or a mining run below 40% hydration.
  • If you are downed and bleeding, hydration keeps draining — a late paramedic may find a corpse.

Interaction with other systems

SystemEffect
SprintingFaster drain on both bars
Downed stateDrain continues while bleeding out
DiseasesSome conditions accelerate hunger loss
WeatherLong exposure to rain contributes to cold

Staff notes

Configuration lives in plugins/MinecitySuite/survival-config.yml. Drain rates, damage interval, HUD style and the per-item restore values are all editable there.
