City Life/Vehicles & Traffic

Vehicles & Traffic

Vehicles are handled by MTVehicles. They are the fastest way across the city and one of the largest money sinks — a car costs money to buy, to fuel and to keep in one piece.

Buying a vehicle

Dealerships around the metropolis sell several classes:

ClassExamplesNotes
SedansFour-door city carsCheap, reliable, unremarkable
Sports carsHigh-performance coupésExpensive, fast, fragile
SUVs / 4x4Off-road vehiclesHandle rough terrain
MotorcyclesSport bikes and choppersFast, no protection
AircraftHelicopters, light planesExpensive, staff-restricted in some areas
WatercraftMotorboats, jet skisDocks and beaches only

Driving controls

InputAction
Right-click the vehicleGet in as driver
Right-click againSwitch seat / passenger
ShiftExit the vehicle
W A S DAccelerate, steer, brake
SpaceHandbrake / jump
Warning

Vehicles take collision and fall damage. Driving a sports car into a wall at full speed is how players discover the repair bill.

Keys and ownership

Every vehicle has a physical key.

  • Only the owner, or someone holding the key, can drive it.
  • Keys can be handed over — that is how you lend a car in roleplay.
  • A stolen vehicle is a crime scene, not a bug. Report it only if a server rule was broken.

Fuel

Vehicles consume fuel while driving.

CommandPurpose
/vehicle fuelShows the remaining fuel
/vehicle benzineRefuels from a fuel can

Refuel at any gas station. Running dry in the middle of a chase is a classic rookie mistake.

Tip

Keep one fuel can in the trunk. It costs almost nothing and it has saved more getaways than any gun.

The trunk

/vehicle trunk

Opens the vehicle's storage. You must be holding the key or sitting in the vehicle.

Items stored in the trunk do not drop if you die outside the car. That makes the trunk the safest place for cash and valuables while travelling.

Garage and recovery

CommandPurpose
/vehicle menuMain menu: buy, spawn, garage
/vehicle garageList vehicles you own
/vehicle restoreRecover a stuck or desynced vehicle

If a vehicle disappears, check the garage before panicking. Vehicles destroyed or lost to a server crash may need staff intervention.

Traffic rules

  1. Do not ram players intentionally. Using a car as a weapon is treated as rule-breaking.
  2. Do not block the hospital, police station or spawn.
  3. Helicopters may fly over the city; landing on someone's roof is not allowed.
  4. Park legally — abandoned vehicles in the road get removed.
  5. Do not drive into active crime scenes for fun.

Costs of ownership

ExpenseWhen
Purchase priceOnce
FuelConstantly
RepairsAfter collisions
Impound / recoveryIf abandoned or destroyed
InsuranceOptional, for expensive vehicles
Note

A cheap sedan that runs is worth more than a wrecked supercar. Buy within your means, then upgrade when your salary tier rises.
