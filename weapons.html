Weapons & Ballistics

Firearms are handled by QualityArmory: real magazines, calibres, iron sights and bullet wounds that need a paramedic, not a bandage.

Where guns come from

SourceWhat you get
Licensed gun stores (Ammu-Nation)Pistols and civilian-legal weapons
The black marketAutomatic weapons, heavy weapons, contraband
Player trading (/ah)Anything someone is willing to sell
Duty kitsPolice and specific roles only
Warning

Carrying a black-market weapon in public is probable cause for a police stop. In roleplay, that is a decision your character makes — and lives with.

Handling a firearm

InputAction
Right-clickAim down iron sights

FReload
Sneak + left-clickAutomatic fire, on supported weapons

Ammunition is calibre-specific. A 9mm round will not load a rifle. Every weapon's lore shows its calibre, magazine size and damage.

Tip

Reload behind cover, not in the open. Reloading cancels if you sprint or swap items.

Gunshot wounds

Bullets do more than remove health — they start a bleeding condition.

  1. A hit can leave a Gunshot Wound.
  2. Bleeding drains health continuously.
  3. After 3 minutes untreated, the wound becomes sepsis, which is far harder to cure.
StageTreatment
BleedingHemostatic Trauma Medkit, or a paramedic
SepsisBroad-Spectrum Antibiotics

Full medical detail is on the EMS & Hospital page.

Important

Do not "walk it off". A gunshot wound is cheap to treat in the first minute and dangerous by the fourth.

Combat rules

  1. No random deathmatch. You need an in-character reason to shoot.
  2. No spawn camping. Shooting players as they spawn is a ban.
  3. No combat logging. Disconnecting mid-fight to avoid death is punished.
  4. Value your life. A gun to your head means compliance, not heroics.
  5. No explosives in crowds. Area damage in a populated area is a fast route to removal.

Ammunition and economy

  • Ammo is bought, found or crafted.
  • Prices rise when the city is in conflict — supply and demand works here.
  • Selling ammo to gangs is profitable and extremely illegal.
